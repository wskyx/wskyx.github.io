So How Did The Aliens Get Here

The distances between points in cosmos can be large. Everyone knows that. So how did the aliens get here? Sci-fi fans should be familiar with the concept of space jumps. But what is a space jump? It’s a way to put matter in a hyper state and push it over the space from a point one to point b. I suspect it requires a lot of energy to make a distance of such. The further you go the more energy one needs. Then how? I suspect conventional sources of energy wont make it. Another thing is we don’t even have a physics formula to even simulate such jump. So what would be the source of energy to perform such jump? I came up with a concept of tesseract, an object that can be found here and there in cosmos, a small object, a source of energy able to power entire planet or power space jumps. Why such a concept? Makes a lot of sense to me since conventional sources of energy barely make our civilisation going. The tesseract concept fits perfectly in the concept of space jump travel. I suspect this is how they go from a stellar system to a stellar system. Why don’t we get invaded by aliens so far? I can only suspect that our system is taken and protected. And if we find a tesseract and learn how to use it it will change humanity entirely for good. But what about the wormholes concept? Well I think that space jumps work good for short to mid distance travel and wormholes are rather meant for larger distance travel. That’s all from me. Have a good one. And hey, maybe you’ll find a tesseract somewhere in your attic :D
